Independence and donor disclosures

  • Financial contributions do not purchase investigative conclusions, influence the correction record, or determine eligibility for advocacy.
  • Public reporting remains accessible regardless of financial contribution.
  • Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.
  • Donations to HAA are not represented as direct financial relief for any specific participant.
  • Advocacy information is not automatically used in an investigation and cannot be published merely because HAA received support.

Current campaign goals, any platform fees, and payment availability are shown on the live Ko-fi page. HAA does not publish donor identity without appropriate permission.
