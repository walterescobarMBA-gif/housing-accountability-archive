October 17, 2026 · Primary-source publication

Redacted LAHD HOPWA monitoring reports available for public download

HAA published downloadable, redacted copies of two Los Angeles Housing Department monitoring reports covering the 2023–2024 and 2024–2025 monitoring cycles. The reports document deficiencies in sampled APLA-direct Housing Specialist participant files while recording no formal findings or concerns.

  • 2023–2024: Significant service and/or case-note gaps in 3 of 10 sampled files; LAHD requested updated policies and procedures.
  • 2024–2025: Missing or incomplete information in 7 of 10 sampled files; LAHD also reported that database enrollment limitations caused underreporting of Crisis and Transitional Housing clients. The report stated there were no prior issues and required no corrective action.
  • Both redacted primary-source PDFs are available from the Records Verification page, alongside HAA's documentary analysis.

Verification question: What follow-up and decision criteria explain the agency's treatment of documented file deficiencies and its subsequent “no prior issues” statement? The samples concern APLA-direct files, not a review of every subcontractor or participant.
