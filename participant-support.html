Participant Support

Help navigating the administrative side of housing.

Housing systems can require participants to manage applications, referrals, documents, provider communications, deadlines, and records while already dealing with housing instability. HAA provides participant-directed navigation and administrative advocacy intended to make those processes easier to understand, document, and move through.

Participant-authorized work

Participant advocacy

Walter Escobar, founder of Housing Accountability Archive, works with participants to document housing decisions and ask the organizations responsible for written answers. This work covers case closures, immediate housing and safety concerns, and the steps toward permanent housing.

Case-closure disputes

With participant authorization, HAA organizes notices and correspondence into a timeline and prepares written disputes. It asks the program to explain why a case was closed, identify the procedure it followed, and describe how the participant can request review.

Safety and continuity of assistance

HAA documents reported safety concerns and gaps in assistance. It asks the responsible provider or agency to identify immediate protective measures, explain how assistance will continue during a transition, and name a contact who can follow up.

Transitions to permanent housing

HAA requests safe interim options and a written plan for permanent placement. It asks the provider to name who will pursue each option, set target dates for decisions and next steps, and explain how it will keep the participant informed.

Consent and confidentiality

Participants direct the work and authorize communications on their behalf. HAA handles individual advocacy separately from public reporting. Publishing confidential participant materials requires separate informed authorization.

How HAA Can Help

Support is organized around the participant’s goals, available information, and administrative next steps.

Navigation

Referral & Application Navigation

Help identifying an appropriate program or referral pathway, accessing applications from a phone or computer, understanding administrative steps, and preparing information needed to complete the process.

Communication

Communication & Follow-Up

Help organizing questions, preparing written status requests, following up with providers, and creating a clearer administrative record when communication becomes difficult.

Advocacy

Administrative Advocacy

At a participant’s request, HAA can help identify unresolved administrative questions, organize supporting information, and communicate concerns clearly to a provider or agency.

Documentation

Records & Documentation Support

Help preserving notices, emails, screenshots, applications, referrals, correspondence, and other administrative records; organizing a chronology; and identifying documentation that may be important to continuity.

Literacy

Process & Records Literacy

Plain-language help understanding housing-program terminology, referrals, notices, supportive services, participant records, and administrative processes.

Pathways

Escalation Pathway Navigation

When ordinary communication does not resolve an issue, HAA can help identify whether the next administrative step may involve a provider supervisor, program sponsor, administering agency, grievance process, records request, oversight channel, or independent legal-services organization.

Participant-Directed Support

The participant remains in control of their own housing decisions and communications. HAA’s role is to help clarify, organize, document, and navigate administrative processes. HAA does not communicate with a provider as a participant’s advocate without the participant’s knowledge and permission.

What HAA Does Not Do

  • HAA is not a housing provider.
  • HAA does not control program eligibility, placement, vouchers, benefits, or funding.
  • HAA does not guarantee housing or program acceptance.
  • HAA is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation or legal advice.
  • HAA is not an emergency or crisis-response service.
  • HAA does not replace an assigned case manager, housing specialist, attorney, medical professional, or government agency.

When an issue requires professional legal representation, emergency assistance, medical services, or another specialized service, HAA may help identify an appropriate external resource.

Start with the minimum information necessary.

Participants should not send Social Security numbers, passwords, full financial-account information, identification documents, medical records, or other highly sensitive information in an initial inquiry unless there is a specific and appropriate reason to do so.
